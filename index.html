Short highlights from Twitch streamers, with permission and credit.

clipper is a small personal tool run by TheClipshere.

What it does

  • Finds popular Twitch clips from streamers who have given permission for their clips to be used.
  • Edits each clip into a vertical short video: captions, a short title card, and a visible "@streamer on Twitch" credit.
  • Videos are scheduled and published automatically, spread through the day. Streamers' removal requests are always handled by a person (see below).
  • Publishes the videos only to my own accounts: YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCTXdGUKrkUTfXL9nWdVZ6Jw) and TikTok (https://www.tiktok.com/@theclipshere0).
  • Every video's description credits the streamer: "Credit: @streamer on Twitch".

What it does not do

  • It does not use clips from streamers who have not given permission.
  • It does not post to anyone else's account and is not offered to other users.
  • It does not sell data or show ads.

Streamers

If you're a streamer featured here and want your clips removed, or you'd like your clips included, email furybsns04@gmail.com. Removal requests are handled promptly, and no new clips of yours will be used after you ask.

Platforms used

clipper uses the official Twitch API to find clips, the YouTube Data API to upload videos and read their view counts, and the TikTok Content Posting and Display APIs to send videos to my TikTok drafts and read their view counts. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
