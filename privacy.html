Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

clipper ("the app") is a personal tool operated by TheClipshere ("I", "me"). It runs on my own computer and publishes videos only to my own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what information it uses and why.

Who the app is for

The app is used only by me, to manage my own accounts. It is not offered to the public and nobody else signs in to it.

Information the app accesses

  • Twitch (public data): public clip information from streamers who gave permission: clip titles, links, view counts, game/category, and the clip videos themselves.
  • YouTube (my account), through the YouTube API Services: permission to upload videos to my channel, and read-only access to the view, like and comment counts of the videos it uploaded.
  • TikTok (my account): my basic profile info (user.info.basic), permission to send videos to my TikTok drafts (video.upload), and read access to my public videos and their view and like counts (video.list).

The app does not collect information about viewers of my videos, and does not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

How the information is used

  • To pick clips, edit them, and automatically upload them to my own accounts.
  • To show me private performance reports (views, likes, views per hour) for my own videos.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on my own computer: the login tokens for my accounts, a local database of clips and upload results, and the video files. Nothing is sent to any server other than Twitch, YouTube/Google and TikTok themselves, and only to carry out the uses above.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share any of this information with anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

Downloaded clips are deleted automatically after 7 days. Records of uploaded videos are kept for my reports until I delete them. You can ask me to remove clips of you at any time (see Contact).

Access to my accounts can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's local token files also ends its access.

Google / YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
